 A LITTLE HELP

Small paws. Strange places.

Wander the meadow and hop across the creek. Hold jump for a higher leap.

Echoes replay your last three seconds, then hold their final pose. Use two together; Q vanishes both and frees their slots. Small I and II marks identify your copies. A striped plate scatters its matching pile. Rebuilding can crush you only when a visible new log appears where you were caught. Level one has no aimed attacks. Later switches react to Echoes too: stay behind cover until their volleys have passed.

Level four mixes logs with linked-switch bridges. Its first log pile leaves the screen, then homes back three seconds later. The shed roof protects you and knocks those logs away. The final flag switch waits at the far end, beyond its snare. Level five introduces bell plates and charging geese: an Echo can ring the bell while you take cover on raised ground. Let the goose break the fence, then cross while your Echo holds the logs open.
